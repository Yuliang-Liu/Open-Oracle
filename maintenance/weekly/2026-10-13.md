- 完整题目：**Relational graphic structure supports diachronic retrieval of wholly unseen ancient Chinese character identities**。
- 作者：Yahui Yang、Yue Qi、Shihui Yang、Zihan Lin、Lejia Zhang、Yan Zhou。
- [官方页面](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-026-03044-y)，DOI `10.1038/s40494-026-03044-y`，Published **6 October 2026**；[官方接收稿 PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-026-03044-y_reference.pdf) 载明 received 3 September / accepted 25 September 2026。
